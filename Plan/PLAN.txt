A high-energy, tactical LAN multiplayer FPS with a vibrant, stylized Valorant/Overwatch
aesthetic, designed for competitive play on local networks and optimized to run at

1. CORE PILLARS & ARCHITECTURAL FOUNDATIONS
================================================================================
PILLAR 1: AAA-FEEL TACTICAL WEAPON ARSENAL & MELEE BLADE
- Slot 1: VANGUARD AR — rapid 30-round automatic assault rifle with glowing holo-sight.
- Slot 2: PHANTOM PISTOL — 3-dot green tritium sights, high single-shot accuracy.
- Slot 3: OPERATOR SNIPER — 95 DMG heavy bolt-action with 22 FOV scope HUD overlay.
- Slot 4: TACTICAL BLADE [NEW GIFT FEATURE] — Serrated tactical knife with glowing Radianite edge:
  * Fast horizontal slash (Left-Click, 55 DMG).
  * Heavy tactical thrust (Right-Click, 100 DMG critical elimination).
  * +20% sprint/movement speed boost while holding knife (Valorant fast rotate mechanic).
  * 360-degree spinning knife flip inspect animation [F].
  * Quick melee access via [4] or [V].
- Visual FX: Fiery muzzle flashes, 120m/s high-speed tracers, 3D floating damage numbers.
- Tactical Smoke Ability [E]: 100% volumetric soft billowing cloud dome with expanding
  Radianite ground shockwave ring and player vision blind vignette inside.

PILLAR 2: EXPANSIVE MULTI-BUILDING URBAN ARENA ("DISTRICT 7 - HAVEN FALL")
- Large 150m x 150m tactical map designed around three competitive lanes:
  * Lane 1 (Site A - Radianite Core): Multi-level defense hub with 3-story A-Tower.
  * Lane 2 (Mid Market / Choke Avenue): Cobblestone streets, archways, and market apartments.
  * Lane 3 (Site B - Industrial Warehouse): High-ceiling warehouse with catwalks.
- Strategic line-of-sight design with realistic choke points, callouts, and flank routes.
- Stylized PBR textures: Cobblestone, slate brick, Tuscan ochre plaster, Radianite panels.

PILLAR 3: LAN MULTIPLAYER & PERFORMANCE ARCHITECTURE
- NetworkManager Autoload (ENetMultiplayerPeer):
  * Zero-setup LAN Host (Port 7777) & Join via IP (127.0.0.1 or Local Wi-Fi/Ethernet).
  * Auto-detection of Host's Local LAN IP for fast party invites.
- Client-Side Prediction & Network Synchronization:
  * 30Hz reliable transform interpolation for ultra-smooth operative movement.
  * Networked RPC combat: bullets, tracers, hitmarkers, smoke grenades, health, and kill feed.
  * Full 3D Operative character model with third-person weapon attachments for remote peers.
- Tactical Scoreboard [Tab] & Settings Menu [Esc]:
  * Dynamic scoreboard showing operatives, ping, kills, deaths.
  * Full in-game sensitivity and volume controls.
- Optimized for Intel i3 / Budget GPUs: GL Compatibility renderer, zero expensive post-fx lag.
